Who we are

Funeral care built on presence, not paperwork.

Awelani Funeral Parlour was founded to make sure no family walks the days after a loss alone. From the first phone call to the final unveiling, one team stays with you — arranging logistics quietly in the background so you're free to grieve, remember, and gather.

  • Dedicated family liaison from day one
  • Collection anywhere in the region, any hour
  • Cultural and religious rites honoured precisely
What we offer

Every arrangement, handled with one call.

From the moment of loss to the months that follow, our services are built to remove the logistics from your hands.

Collection & transport

Respectful, prompt collection from home, hospital or roadside, available around the clock.

Memorial & funeral services

Full coordination of church, community hall or graveside services, on your schedule.

Casket & preparation

A considered range of caskets and dignified preparation, matched to your wishes and budget.

Repatriation

Bringing loved ones home across provinces or borders, with all documentation managed for you.

Grave & tombstone services

Grave preparation, tent and catering for the day, and tombstone unveiling arrangements.

After-care support

Guidance on estate documents, grief support referrals, and check-ins in the weeks that follow.
